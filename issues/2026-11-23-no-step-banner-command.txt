What failed: flows draw a "STEP 2/5 ✓ PASS ..." banner with an eval'd window.pwStep()
that inserts a div. A location.reload() (or any full navigation) drops the div and the
helper, so later steps either lose the banner or fail calling pwStep.

Suggested fix: a `step [pass|fail] <text>` command that draws the banner and re-draws it
after every navigation, until the next `step` or `step off`.

## STATUS 2026-11-23 — FIXED in pw_trace.js
`step <text>`, `step pass <text>`, `step off` added. The kit keeps the text and redraws it on
every domcontentloaded; pointer-events:none; on a failed step it turns red with the line and
command. Proven: tests/gap_fixes.js (survives location.reload, does not block a click under it,
red at the failing line) and live 17 + 14 (survived reload).
